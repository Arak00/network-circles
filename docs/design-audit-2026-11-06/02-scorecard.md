# Scorecard

1. Good design is innovative: 2/3
   Evidence: Layers view as a layer diagram, who-do-I-know run on your own export inside the tab (E2, E6).
   Justification: a clear improvement on InMaps-style maps, but the map itself is the familiar force-directed hairball.

2. Good design makes a product useful: 1/3
   Evidence: the primary job's box is in the side panel, last in focus order and about 1,000 px down on a phone; clicking a result zooms into grey discs (E1, E2).
   Justification: the job can be done, but only after detours, so not 2.

3. Good design is aesthetic: 1/3
   Evidence: one token system (E4), but 77% to 97% of nodes overlap, names sit on discs, the tooltip covers the toolbar (E1, E2).
   Justification: the overlapping blobs are a jarring violation on every screen with a big circle.

4. Good design makes a product understandable: 1/3
   Evidence: two search boxes, and "circles", "cohesion", "unplaced", "fold" and "Layers" are unexplained on screen (E2).
   Justification: more than one control needs help, and the jargon sits next to the numbers.

5. Good design is unobtrusive: 1/3
   Evidence: header, file banner, toolbar, hint line, timeline and four side cards all show at once (E2).
   Justification: the chrome competes with the content; it does not dominate it, so not 0.

6. Good design is honest: 2/3
   Evidence: the upload promise is tested (E5); before the data fix, a "Freelance" circle contradicted "people linked by a shared company" (E3, E5).
   Justification: one label/behaviour mismatch, now fixed at the source.

7. Good design is long-lasting: 3/3
   Evidence: system font, flat tokens, no trend styling (E4).
   Justification: nothing dates it.

8. Good design is thorough down to the last detail: 2/3
   Evidence: all six states exist (E4), but the keyboard tooltip lands in the wrong place and is clipped, and the timeline breaks when connections cluster in one month (E2).
   Justification: states are covered; two details are rough.

9. Good design is environmentally friendly: 2/3
   Evidence: about 470 KB of JS, motion respects the preference, dark mode honoured; 21 s blocking layout at 3,000 people (E6).
   Justification: under 500 KB with motion gated, but the large-network freeze rules out 3.

10. Good design is as little design as possible: 1/3
    Evidence: removable or mergeable elements: the second search box, the hint line, the file-path source line, the cohesion numbers, the first-visit modal, the Show card in the Map view (E2).
    Justification: three to five removable elements.

Total: 16/30. No principle scored 0.
