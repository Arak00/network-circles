# Recruiting screener

Use this to pick interviewees for the switch interviews in `interview-guide.md`. It selects people by something that happened to them recently, not by job title, because the moment is what makes someone reach for their network. Send it as a short form; it takes about two minutes.

## Quotas

Aim for six interviews per trigger you are testing, eight at most. Start with the triggers that `findings.md` ranks highest. Six people who went through the same moment are usually enough to see the pattern repeat; if the sixth interview still brings a new kind of story, add two more.

| Trigger | Qualifies when the answer to Q3 includes |
|---------|------------------------------------------|
| Job search | a or b |
| Fundraising or selling | c |
| After an event | d |
| Keeping in touch | e |

## Questions

Q1. How many connections do you have on LinkedIn, roughly?
- Under 100 → end politely (too few for circles to mean anything)
- 100 to 499
- 500 to 1,999
- 2,000 or more

Q2. Do you work in user research, UX or product design, or at LinkedIn or a company that makes CRM or contact-management software?
- Yes → end politely (professional bias)
- No

Q3. In the last three months, which of these happened to you? Pick all that apply.
- a. I lost my job, or I started looking for a new one
- b. I started a new job
- c. I started raising money, or started looking for clients or customers
- d. I went to a conference, meetup or event where I met new people
- e. I realised I had lost touch with people I wanted to stay in touch with
- f. None of these → end politely

Q4. For the one you ticked that mattered most to you: what did you actually do about it in the first two weeks? (free text)
Look for a concrete action: a search, a list, a message, a tool. "Nothing yet" is fine for e, not for a to d. Vague answers ("networked a lot") are a weak sign; prefer people who name what they did.

Q5. Which of these have you used in the last year? Pick all that apply.
- A spreadsheet of contacts or target companies
- LinkedIn search or filters to find people
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Recruiter
- A personal CRM (for example Folk, Dex, Clay, Notion templates)
- Exported my LinkedIn data
- None of these
This is for context, not for screening out. Try to get a mix.

Q6. Are you willing to talk for 30 minutes on a video call about that moment? We won't ask for names of anyone you know, and we won't look at your LinkedIn data.
- Yes → ask for a contact address and two time slots
- No → end politely

## What not to ask

Don't describe the tool or show it before the interview. Don't ask whether they would use a network map. Both prime the answers the interview is meant to collect.
