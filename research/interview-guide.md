# Switch interview guide (30 minutes)

The goal is to reconstruct one recent moment in which the person needed their network, step by step, from what they actually did. Every question is about the past. People are poor at predicting what they would do and good at remembering what they did, especially when you ask for details: where they were, what was on the screen, who they messaged.

Two people if you can: one asks, one takes notes. Record only with consent, and never write down names of the people they mention; use "a former colleague", "an investor" and so on.

## 1. Opening (2 minutes)

"Thanks for doing this. I'm not testing you and there are no right answers. I want to understand one real situation in detail. I won't ask for the names of anyone you know. Is it OK if I record the audio, just for my notes?"

## 2. Context (3 minutes)

- What do you do, and how do you use LinkedIn on a normal week?
- In the screener you mentioned [the moment from Q3/Q4]. When was that?

## 3. The timeline (20 minutes)

Walk through it in order. Ask for specifics at each step and let silences run.

First thought
- When did you first realise you needed people for this? Where were you? What happened just before?

Looking around
- What was the first thing you did? Show me, or describe the screen.
- Who did you think of first? How did you come up with those people?
- Did you look anything up, make a list, open a spreadsheet, search LinkedIn? What exactly did you type or filter on?

Getting stuck
- What was harder or slower than you expected?
- Was there anyone you knew you should contact but couldn't find or couldn't remember?
- Did you try something that didn't work? What did you do instead?

Deciding and doing
- How did you decide who to contact first?
- What did you actually send? How many people? What happened?

Afterwards
- What did you keep from this: a list, notes, a tool? Have you opened it since?
- If this happened again next month, what would you do the same way? (This is still about their own process, not about our product.)

## 4. Spend and workaround (3 minutes)

- Have you paid for anything to help with this, or spent a lot of time on it? How much?
- Is there something you do regularly now because of this, like a weekly habit or a reminder?

## 5. Close (2 minutes)

- Is there anything about that moment I should have asked and didn't?
- Thank them. If they ask about the tool, show it only now, after the interview is over, and don't count anything they say about it as evidence.

## Questions not to ask

- "Would you use…?", "Would it help if…?", "How much would you pay for…?" These invite polite guesses.
- Any description of the tool before section 5.
- Leading questions such as "Wasn't it frustrating that LinkedIn doesn't show…?"
