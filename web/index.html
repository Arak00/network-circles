How Network Circles works

1. Get your files from LinkedIn

  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose the larger data archive. The smaller one has no skills, endorsements or posts.
  3. LinkedIn emails you a link within a day. Download the zip and unzip it.

2. Open them here

  • Click Open your LinkedIn files and select all the CSV files, or drop them on the page.
  • Everything runs in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

3. Read the map

  • Each dot is a connection. Colours are circles: people linked by a shared company or added around the same days.
  • Hover anything to see what it connects to. Click to keep it highlighted, click the background to let go.
  • Layers lines up your groups, topics, posts and skills on the left and organisations and events on the right of your circles.
  • Drag the timeline or press Play to watch your network grow.
